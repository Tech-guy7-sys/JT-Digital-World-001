J TECH DIGITAL WORLD

Software built around your work.

JT Digital World is an ecosystem of focused applications connected by a common product identity and AI layer.

THE VISION

Build useful software that feels connected.

JT Digital World brings together desktop assistance, creative production, coding, storage, app management and live production under one family of products.

The goal is not to make every application look or behave the same. The goal is to make them feel like parts of one coherent environment.

THE AI EXPERIENCE

Jeff AI belongs inside the work.

Jeff AI is the user-facing assistant across JT applications. It can suggest, explain, create and assist according to the context and permissions of the app.

Tutorials are intentionally kept inside each individual application, where they can teach the exact tools users are working with.

THE ECOSYSTEM

Nine focused products.

From desktop automation to live broadcasting, the JT family is designed to cover different types of modern digital work.

Create

JT Motion, JT Studio and JT Design provide dedicated creative environments.

Build

JT Code and Agent Jeff support development, automation and technical workflows.

Connect

JT Cloud and the shared JT core provide infrastructure for projects and connected workflows.

Broadcast

JT Stream brings scenes, sources, recording and supported live outputs into the JT family.

Manage

JT App Manager handles installation, updates, repair and diagnostics across the ecosystem.

Think

Jeff AI provides the dedicated AI workspace while Jeff AI is also embedded throughout the other apps.
