JT Digital World v44.0.0 — Rich Multi-Page Website

This release expands every public website section so pages are substantive rather than sparse.
Home remains focused. Apps, Features, About, Support, Download and Sign In are dedicated pages.
Tutorials remain inside the individual JT desktop apps; the public website does not host product tutorials.
Public pricing/offers remain out of the website.

Rich pages include:
- Apps: nine products, previews, app capabilities, Jeff AI/in-app tutorial badges, connected workflow section.
- Features: six major capability areas plus platform experience and control sections.
- About: direction, Jeff AI philosophy, ecosystem roles and product-family overview.
- Support: help paths, account, installation, contact, status and diagnostics.
- Download: desktop release information, channels, install/sign-in/manage workflow.
- Sign In: account purpose, access, connected services, security and settings information.
- Home: focused hero, ecosystem introduction and three concise platform pillars; no full-page content dump.
